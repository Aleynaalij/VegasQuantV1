# NBA editorial selections

On October 19, 2026 the owner requested publication of supported opening-day NBA picks now. This authorizes early qualitative editorial selections, superseding the preparation-only calendar wording for this publication. It does not approve the shadow model or authorize official/qualifying-edge picks.

`nba_editorial_picks` stores immutable individual selections linked to `nba_games` and exact `nba_observations`. It deliberately does not use NFL `games`, `candidate_releases`, challenge stages or result records. The insert guard verifies a future regular-season game, full-game points/rebounds market, exact FanDuel player/direction/line/price, an observation at most eight hours old, and documented role/availability review. This eight-hour cap allows the existing six-hour collection cadence; the displayed price is always an observed snapshot, never represented as executable/live. Quote/provider and receipt timestamps are copied from the immutable observation. Publication time is assigned by the database. No odds are synthesized.

The table has member/age RLS matching NBA observations, read-only client grants, and service-role insert permission. No client publisher or security-definer mutation RPC is introduced. All updates/deletes are prohibited. New material evidence or price changes require a new publication key and a same-game/player/market `supersedes_id`; the original survives. A selection has one original and a linear successor chain; revisions must supersede the current row. Independent duplicate originals and branches are rejected. No notification messages are sent by inserts.

The NBA Zone reads these rows and displays shared Vegas Quant playing cards, the case, risk, source/availability details, original observed price and existing Gambly clipboard/open flow. Copying closes at the earlier of original expiry and current tipoff, or on live/final/postponed/canceled status or a loaded superseding publication. The card can still be downloaded for reference. These selections do not appear as official, do not create model probability/EV claims, and do not change Home POD/Hail Mary, tails, bankrolls, NFL publishing, provider settings, schedules or grades.

The insert guards require historical cutoff, generation time, at least two source records with HTTPS URLs, SHA-256 hashes and retrieval times, role/availability review, the exact quote observation ID and a matching canonical input hash. The hash is SHA-256 over PostgreSQL jsonb text after removing input_sha256 and input_hash_format. Each source must have been retrieved no later than evidence generation. Displayed citations come directly from the validated source records, with labels matched by exact URL; an unrelated top-level citation cannot become a displayed evidence link. These structural checks preserve provenance; an analyst still verifies source content and the factual case. The NBA shadow model remains SHADOW_ONLY. Published case and risk are analyst inference; historical sample counts are descriptive, not forecasts or validated edges.


## October 31 live rollout: one journal and one Zone

The owner's live rollout supersedes the paused product state. The original journal guards, evidence hash/timeline, exact FanDuel quote and revision rules remain in force; no shadow model is approved. New private `publish_nba_editorial_zone(payload, expected_author)` wraps that existing insert with one matching Community/Zone post in the same transaction. It is SECURITY INVOKER with EXECUTE revoked from anon, authenticated and service_role. Trusted server SQL must supply the independently verified expected owner; the configured original card author must match, with a ready profile/avatar and age eligibility. Existing Community post guards run as that author, and caller claims are restored afterward. A stable publication key and transaction lock prevent retry duplicates; a failed Zone insert rolls back the journal insertion. No client publisher, new job, notification channel, stake or modeled edge is introduced. Existing NBA review/collection schedules continue; publication remains evidence-driven and a pass is valid.

Tests in `tests/sql/nba-editorial-zone.mjs` cover wrong owner, incomplete profile/age, exact-price failure, atomic rollback, duplicate retry, immutable-key mismatch and author restoration. A new publication still requires actual sourced role/availability analysis. Incomplete team injury submissions are an explicit coverage gap, not clearance.
