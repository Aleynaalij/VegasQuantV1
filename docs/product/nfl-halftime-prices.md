# NFL live and halftime FanDuel prices

The owner authorized halftime price collection on October 22, 2026. The separate `nfl-live-props` Edge Function keeps the existing pregame collector, spend settings and publication gates unchanged. A new five-minute Cron job invokes it with the existing source-sync Vault token, verified by `feed_authorize`.

It requests only FanDuel and six full-game player markets. The free events endpoint supplies live event IDs; exact teams and kickoff (within one minute) must match one known NFL game. Started games older than six hours are excluded. At most two events are queried each run, least recently attempted first. Large overlapping slates therefore have longer refresh intervals and reported deferrals.

Only market-level timestamps received after kickoff, at most two minutes old and not future-dated, are accepted. Responses must match the requested event identity. Quote receipts, exact raw response SHA256, provider times and `quote_phase: in_play` are retained in immutable source observations under `The Odds API live props`. Existing pregame observations are preserved. Repeated prices remain timestamped observations; a historical pregame quote is never relabeled live. Missing, suspended, unavailable and stale markets remain unknown. The feed does not establish an executable offer or combined parlay price. These are full-game totals updated during play, not second-half-only props.

`feed_runs` records attempts, inserted quotes, stale/missing FanDuel events, errors, deferred events and provider credit headers. Atomic claims reject overlapping/repeated workers. Local additional-collector limits are 600 reserved credits per rolling day and 4,000 per rolling 30 days. Each run reserves twelve credits (two events times six markets) and reconciles to actual returned usage headers; uncertain costs remain conservatively reserved. The collector stops quoting when the reported provider balance is missing or below 100. These are local safeguards, not a change to the provider plan or a statement of its allowance.

At halftime the research agent can invoke the same authenticated function through `pg_net` and the existing Vault token without reading or disclosing it, then read `feed_runs` and fresh FanDuel source observations. Claims permit another request after one minute (or three minutes for an interrupted running worker). No browser/login/API key from the owner is needed. Research must also inspect the independently stored Sportradar evidence. Neither collector publishes selections or bypasses official/model/pregame gates. No numerical edge, clearance, live availability or parlay payout is inferred.

Deployment/read-back before kickoff can verify authentication, scheduling and no-live-game behavior. Actual FanDuel in-play coverage for tonight is unverified until post-kickoff quotes arrive. Official provider documentation: https://the-odds-api.com/liveapi/guides/v4/index.html .
