# Member parlay builder

The Zone → Parlays exposes Create parlay and Your parlays. The Zone → Legs is the current research-week catalogue (Tuesday noon Eastern rollover), including individual leaves of published tickets. Newest identical game/player/stat observations take precedence. Closed results stay visible; they cannot be added to new combinations. Aggregate ticket rows are not selectable legs.

Members choose two or more upcoming full-game player-stat legs with no leg-count cap. This is a private member combination, separate from editorial R6’s 3–4-leg publication rule. It is not an analyst endorsement, a wager, a tail, an OFFICIAL publication, a qualifying-edge release or a result. No combined price is inferred, particularly for correlated same-game legs. Copy contains only player name and stat line per line.

`create_member_parlay` accepts only a title and published leg keys. The private implementation binds ownership to `auth.uid()`, applies existing membership/age eligibility, validates upcoming NFL games and open selections, rejects duplicates/conflicting player-stat targets and derives immutable leg snapshots from hosted published records. Identical combinations are idempotent. No client-provided odds or selections are trusted. Owners alone can read/delete their rows; auth-user deletion cascades. Direct inserts/updates are denied. Original analyst tickets and publishing gates are unchanged.

`published_leg_catalog` follows the original research access policies. Its recommendation badge is derived server-side from an accepted publishing-gate decision, a model approved before publication, the reviewed probability and exact price, and a quote within 15 minutes. Edge must be at least 0.03 in probability units (three percentage points). SHADOW_ONLY/experimental evidence, missing approvals, unknown prices and stale/future quotes receive no badge. The client also expires a displayed badge as its quote ages. Historic publication and price snapshots remain visible with their original provenance; a missing badge does not imply that an old card was revalidated.

Ongoing research publishes only through the existing selection gate. Watchlist notes do not become selectable recommendations merely to fill the library. There are currently no approved models; the badge infrastructure does not grant model approval.

Verification: helper tests cover exact Gambly copy, weekly deduplication, conflicts and expiry. PGlite tests exercise ownership, forbidden direct writes, authoritative snapshots, idempotency, missing models, shadow evidence and stale quotes. Browser checks cover phone/desktop creation, save/readback, library display, absent false badges and overflow.
