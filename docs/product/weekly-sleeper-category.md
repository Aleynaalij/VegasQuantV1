No odds, sportsbook-price, payout, probability, numerical confidence/edge or approved-model claims are allowed. Other selections, official releases, Research Board and Double Up keep their own gates. No model is approved by this change.

Use existing immutable `candidate_releases`, the documented `ENTERTAINMENT_SLEEPER_PARLAY`/`ENTERTAINMENT_SLEEPER` kinds, `worth_a_dollar:true` on the first bundle only, and `pick_of_day:false,fun_play:false` throughout. Dates precede `historical_cutoff`; `generated_at` must be within one hour. History is context, not an estimated probability. Tight ends use receptions. Confirm actual role/roster against current primary sources; historical team labels and absence from injury lists are not clearance.

Each leaf needs visible why, risk and quote_context explaining unknown executable market availability. Analyst source truth and support still require review; structural checks cannot authenticate arbitrary claims. Do not publish unsupported candidates merely by labeling uncertainty.

A revised release uses a later v-number and links the original through the bundle's `supersedes_release_id`; preserve originals and tails. All games must be in the same week/season, pregame and within seven days. `expires_at` equals the earliest included kickoff. `sweat_until` spans the latest included kickoff, at most 12 hours beyond it. Never backfill a missed pregame card. Successful releases receive an immutable category review; normal notification side effects remain transactional.
