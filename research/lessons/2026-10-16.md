## 2. What we did wrong

1. **Engram: we published a leg our own research said was below break-even.** Its risk note read "only three of five games cleared 15 yards" (60%). At -240 the break-even rate is 71%. The note was honest, but the selection ignored it. The market also moved away from it before kickoff (-240 to -220), and it missed by 2 yards. **This one leg cost the six-leg parlay.**
2. **Low tight-end yardage targets are coin flips in disguise.** Both 15+ tight-end legs were decided by a single catch: Engram missed by 2 and Barner cleared by 1. A "safe-looking" low target is fragile when the player gets only 2-3 targets.
3. **Halftime legs stretched for bigger targets in a script that worked against them.** Denver led by 11 at the half.
   - Dobbins was moved from 30+ before the game to 50+ at halftime, needing 15 more yards. He got 6. Second-half yards for a back are thin even in a positive script, and this target needed 43% of his first-half output again.
   - Harvey, a running back on a receiving over, needed 9 more and got 0, because the leading team ran the clock and threw less to its backs. **The leg's own risk note named exactly this risk.**
4. **The Pick of the Day was priced at -850.** It won, but the bible's expected-value rules need at least 3% edge. At -850 that means a true 92%+ chance to earn about 0.12 units, and one loss erases nine wins. That is an anchor, not a Pick of the Day.
5. **Two parlays on one game, five and six legs, with no priced expected value on any leg.** The bible says to favour straight bets and that parlays should only contain positive-EV legs. Our model is still "shadow only", so no leg carried a validated probability.

What this tells us:
- **Our edge is in alternate thresholds set well below a player's typical output, priced -150 to -249.** That band made essentially all of our profit.
- **Near-even-money props lose.** These are main lines, unders and halftime stretch targets, where we have no model edge over the book: 4-9 so far, and 7 of those 9 losses came at halftime or on unders.
- **Heavy chalk wins but barely pays**, and its prices drifted cheaper before close on 2 of 4 tracked legs (no closing-line value).

**Sample-size caution:** 46 priced legs is small. Treat these as guardrails, not proven edges, and re-measure every week.

## 4. Rules for the next card

These are added to the standing rules in [README.md](README.md).

- **R1 — Clear rate must beat the price.** The player's last-5+ clear rate must be at least break-even plus 5 points. Break-even for a minus price is |price| / (|price| + 100): -150 = 60%, -200 = 67%, -250 = 71%, -300 = 75%. If the risk note shows a lower clear rate, drop the leg.
- **R2 — Live in the -150 to -249 band.** No near-even-money props (-149 or longer) unless a priced model probability shows at least 3% edge.
- **R3 — The Pick of the Day is priced -150 to -300.** Legs at -500 or shorter are parlay anchors only.
- **R4 — Halftime discipline:**
  - At most 3 legs, overs only, priced -150 to -249.
  - No receiving overs for a team leading by 10 or more.
  - No rushing overs that need more than 30% of the player's first-half yards in the second half.
  - No unders.
- **R5 — Tight ends: use receptions** (6-0 so far) instead of 15+ yardage, or skip.
- **R6 — Parlays: 3-4 legs, and every leg passes R1-R5 on its own.**
- **R7 — Price check at lock:** a 15+ cent move against us means drop or replace the leg.
- **R8 — Record hygiene:**
  - Every leg needs an exact price at publish (22 graded legs had none).
  - Never publish the same pick twice: James Cook's 86.5+ rushing yards on Oct 4 is recorded twice.

## 5. Checklist before publishing a leg

- [ ] Exact book price recorded, and the target is an alternate line below the player's median.
- [ ] Last-5+ clear rate is at least break-even + 5 points (R1).
- [ ] Price is between -150 and -249, or it is a declared anchor (R2, R3).
- [ ] The risk note does not describe the most likely game script (R4). If it does, skip.
- [ ] Tight end? Use receptions (R5).
- [ ] In a parlay? The leg passes on its own, and the parlay has 4 legs or fewer (R6).
- [ ] Price re-checked within 2 hours of kickoff (R7).
