VQ-NBA-01 — Separate editorial targets from numerical pricing.
Use PREPARATION, WATCH, QUALITATIVE_EDITORIAL, SHADOW_ONLY, and VALIDATED_MODEL as distinct conceptual states; these labels are not assumed to be installed database enums. A defensible unpriced target may later be editorially liked under owner policy, without BETTABLE, OFFICIAL, validated EV, or a numerical probability. Missing odds remain null. Unknown role or availability can still block a selection even when odds are not required.

VQ-NBA-02 — Keep probability edge and EV separate.
probability_edge_pp = 100 * (p_model - p_market_no_vig).
expected_return_pct = 100 * (p_win * (decimal_odds - 1) - p_loss).
With no push, this reduces to 100 * (p_win * decimal_odds - 1).
At -110, a hypothetical 55% win probability produces 5% EV; against a symmetric 50% no-vig benchmark it is a 5-percentage-point probability edge. The identical numbers in this example are coincidental. Neither formula establishes that the assumed 55% is valid.
The Bible's R-07 thresholds are proposed EV thresholds, not percentage-point edges. They must not silently replace Vegas Quant's existing qualifying-edge gate. Do not lower the platform's three-percentage-point threshold without separate authorization and NBA-specific validation.

VQ-NBA-03 — Fit distributions; do not select them by stat name alone.
A Poisson count assumes equal conditional mean and variance. Minutes uncertainty, role mixtures, matchup, foul trouble and shot attempts can violate that assumption. Compare Poisson, negative binomial, empirical and other justified models on held-out data. Model made threes conditional on uncertain attempts and shooting success where defensible. Points are integer, nonnegative and often skewed; a normal approximation is a baseline to test, not an automatic tail model. Validate alternate-line tails separately.

VQ-NBA-04 — Project minutes as scenarios.
Allocate 240 regulation team-minutes. Handle overtime separately. Use active/limited/out scenarios, rotation shares, first-return restrictions, foul trouble, substitutions and blowout regimes. Do not invent probability-of-playing numbers from injury labels. If scenario weights are unsupported, report scenarios without assigning numeric weights. Verify minutes redistribution from actual roles and observed rotation evidence.

VQ-NBA-05 — Avoid double counting adjustments.
Document whether player rates already incorporate pace, lineup role and opponent quality. Apply each adjustment once. Keep player-impact ratings on compatible scales and include lineup interactions where supported. Multiplying player impact by minutes/48 is a baseline aggregation, not proof of predictive calibration.

VQ-NBA-06 — Audit both time and availability of information.
Record source publication time, provider quote time, retrieval time, forecast generation time and historical cutoff separately. Store source hash, input hash, model/configuration version, season, event/player identifiers, market definition and blockers. A recent retrieval of an old quote is not a fresh quote. No result-era inputs may enter pregame forecasts. Preserved source observations are required for reproducible claims.

VQ-NBA-07 — Grade comparable CLV and prediction quality.
Compare the same player, market, period, line and settlement terms. Price-only CLV needs the same line. When lines differ, use a supported distribution or report line movement separately. Distinguish market-based closing EV from independently validated true EV. CLV is useful evidence, not proof of future profit, especially on thin props and noisy or incomplete closes. Track calibration, Brier score/log loss, interval coverage, forecast error, CLV and realized results together. Cluster evaluation by game/date where dependence exists.

VQ-NBA-08 — Treat uncertainty as part of the decision.
Test sensitivity to minutes, usage, availability and pace. Numerical model weights, home court, rest/referee adjustments and decision thresholds need documented estimates and out-of-sample support. The Bible's suggested weights and constants are starting hypotheses. Do not turn those defaults into claimed trained parameters. Multiple candidate searches require a frozen holdout or prospectively locked evaluation to avoid selection bias.

VQ-NBA-09 — Price a parlay as a joint event.
Positive EV for each single does not guarantee positive EV for a sportsbook's quoted same-game parlay. Use the exact combined executable price and a supported joint outcome distribution. Do not multiply individual probabilities when legs share minutes, pace, usage, teammate competition, foul trouble or blowout risk. Correlation can help or hurt and can already be priced in. If combined pricing or joint modeling is missing, a grouped card is qualitative entertainment, without a combined EV claim. Do not force five, six or seven legs.

VQ-NBA-10 — State settlement terms and pushes.
Separate full-game from remaining-game, half, quarter, regulation and overtime-inclusive markets. Know minimum participation, void, injury protection and stat-correction rules for the actual book. Whole-number lines need explicit p_win, p_push and p_loss. The pick'em equal-per-leg breakeven formula only applies to independent, equal-probability legs and an all-or-nothing payout; flex entries, voids, discounts and correlation require separate calculations.

VQ-NBA-11 — Define halftime opportunity, not proximity.
Record score, clock, possessions, active lineup, actual first-half minutes, fouls, usage, shots, rebound chances, potential assists, injury changes and substitutions. Compute additional production needed only after establishing plausible remaining minutes and opportunity. Separate observed opportunity from actual conversion. One catch/one basket away is not itself evidence of a good leg. A single explosive play is not a repeatable workload. Distinguish full-game thresholds from second-half-only markets. Include shared failure paths and halftime latency. No live selections based on a frozen old snapshot.

VQ-NBA-12 — Use freshness rules appropriate to the state.
The Bible's two-minute odds and 90-minute news checks are proposed operating safeguards, not established provider capabilities. They do not make an unavailable feed fresh. Recheck after material news, lineup changes, suspension/resumption and between request and price observation. Live data needs score/clock/source synchronization and supported latency controls; a generic two-minute rule can be far too stale live. Unsupported feeds keep numerical pricing on WAIT.

VQ-NBA-13 — Keep facts, inference and incentives distinct.
Books forecast outcomes as part of pricing risk; 'books do not predict games' is too absolute. Ticket/handle splits and reverse movement cannot independently prove sharp action or a trap. Crew tendencies, motivation, TV, contracts and draft incentives are hypotheses requiring evidence, not fixed adjustments. Do not automatically strip every 'tanking' game: define any filtering rule in advance, test it and avoid removing inconvenient observations. A changed rule does not prove fewer blowouts or a specific minutes effect.

VQ-NBA-14 — Protect research from unsupported numerical examples.
The source's simulated drawdowns, sample-size requirements and growth figures require runnable code, random seed, assumptions, payoff definition and statistical design before use as verified results. A confidence-level calculation is not a power calculation; dependence and model selection change the required sample. -110/-110 has 4.7619% overround and 4.5455% normalized theoretical margin. That is not a guaranteed realized sportsbook hold. The source's fair-leg parlay loss examples assume independent 50%-probability outcomes paid by multiplying -110 decimal prices; they are not universal SGP hold estimates.
No Kelly sizing without a defensible probability estimate and uncertainty treatment. Exposure means total cash at risk across correlated positions, not counting a group as a single small bet. Do not alter user bankrolls or place wagers from this reference.

VQ-NBA-15 — Version and verify external policy.
Season dates, competition rules, injury-report deadlines, prop restrictions, laws, court decisions, taxes, provider prices and commercial redistribution rights are dated claims. Maintain a fact register with official URL, effective date, verification time and refresh trigger. Do not infer legal permission to scrape or redistribute from public accessibility. Tax, legal, commercial and venue claims in the original are not adopted as verified guidance by this review.
