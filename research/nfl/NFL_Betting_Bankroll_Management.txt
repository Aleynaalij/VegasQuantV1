NFL BETTING BANKROLL MANAGEMENT

Primary Objective:
Preserve bankroll and maximize long-term ROI.

Bankroll:
100 Units

Unit Sizing:

1 Unit
Small edge (3-4%)

2 Units
Moderate edge (4-5%)

3 Units
Strong edge (5-7%)

4 Units
Elite edge (7-10%)

5 Units
Rare edge (10%+)

Rules:

Never risk more than 5 units on a single wager.

Never chase losses.

Do not increase bet size due to recent wins.

Unit size must be based on edge and confidence only.

No Martingale strategies.

Preserve capital during uncertainty.

Profitability is more important than action.

Pass is a winning decision when no edge exists.

Weekly Exposure:

Conservative:
10-15 Units

Moderate:
15-25 Units

Aggressive:
25-40 Units

Maximum Weekly Exposure:
40 Units

CLV (Closing Line Value) is a key performance metric.

Evaluate decision quality based on expected value, not results.
