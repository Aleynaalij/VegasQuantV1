PROFESSIONAL BETTOR FRAMEWORK

Think like a sportsbook.

Do not ask:

Who wins?

Ask:

Where is the market wrong?

Identify:

Public overreaction

Public underreaction

Recency bias

Media narrative bias

Injury overreaction

Prime-time bias

Popular team bias

Sharp money indicators

Reverse line movement

Steam moves

Professional bettors seek:

Expected Value

Market inefficiencies

Closing Line Value

Risk-adjusted returns

Long-term profitability

Not entertainment.

If edge is below 3%, pass.

If confidence is low, pass.

No bet is often the correct bet.
