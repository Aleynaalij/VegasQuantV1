VEGAS MINDSET FRAMEWORK

Primary Rule:

Never ask:

Who will win?

Always ask:

What outcome would hurt the sportsbook the most?

=====================================

SPORTSBOOK THINKING

Oddsmakers are not trying to predict games.

Oddsmakers are trying to:

1. Price risk.

2. Balance liability.

3. Exploit public perception.

4. Create efficient markets.

5. Encourage action on both sides.

=====================================

PUBLIC BETTOR PROFILE

Public bettors tend to prefer:

Favorite teams

Popular quarterbacks

Recent winners

Overs

Prime-time teams

Media narratives

Star players

Large favorites

Public money often overreacts to:

Final scores

Highlight plays

Injuries

Media stories

Winning streaks

=====================================

SHARP BETTOR PROFILE

Sharp bettors seek:

Mispriced lines

Overreactions

Injury inflation

Line value

Reverse line movement

Market mistakes

Expected value

Closing line value

Professional bettors frequently take positions that appear uncomfortable.

=====================================

SPORTSBOOK FEAR CHECKLIST

Determine:

Which side most casual bettors will choose.

Which side professional bettors will choose.

Which side creates sportsbook liability.

Which side appears unpopular yet statistically attractive.

=====================================

REVERSE LINE MOVEMENT

If:

Majority of bets are on Team A

and

Line moves toward Team B

Flag:

Potential Sharp Money Signal

=====================================

TRAP LINE INVESTIGATION

Search for:

Lines that appear too easy.

Favorites that seem obvious.

Narratives everyone agrees with.

Questions:

Why is the line not higher?

Why is the market resisting movement?

What information is already priced in?

=====================================

BOOK LIABILITY MODEL

For every game determine:

PUBLIC SIDE

SHARP SIDE

BOOK LIABILITY SIDE

MOST DANGEROUS WAGER

SIDE MOST LIKELY TO BE MISPRICED

=====================================

VEGAS FEAR INDEX SCORE

10 = Sportsbook likely exposed

8-9 = Significant sharp interest

6-7 = Moderate sharp interest

4-5 = Balanced market

1-3 = Little concern
